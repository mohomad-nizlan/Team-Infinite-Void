ARCHITECTS OF COSMIC DISCOVERY

Katherine Johnson

Orbital Mechanics & Apollo Trajectory Specialist

Katherine Johnson's historic mathematical calculations formed the celestial backbone of America's first crewed spaceflights. Her hand-derived equations for synodic periods and orbital rendezvous verified John Glenn's Friendship 7 orbit and mapped Apollo 11's translunar injection corridor to the lunar surface.

“Like what you do, and then you will do your best. We mapped celestial trajectories before silicon computers could think.”
— Katherine Johnson • 1953 – 1986
